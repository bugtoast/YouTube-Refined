# YouTube Refined

YouTube Refined lets you tailor a few parts of YouTube to your liking:

- Choose how many videos appear in each row, from 1 to 10 (or toggle off to use YouTube’s native responsive grid).
- Hide Shorts from YouTube. Shorts links take you to the homepage.
- Hide “Explore more topics” sections in your feed.
- Hide “Get more from memberships” sections in your feed.
- Hide Mix playlists from your feed.
- Hide the filter chip bar (All, Gaming, Music…) from your feed.
- Filter live streams, started videos, and playlist cards from feeds.
- Hide the left navigation sidebar.
- Hide comments, the watch-page sidebar, or end-screen cards.

Your settings are saved and stay in sync across Chrome.

## Install

1. Open `chrome://extensions` in Chrome.
2. Turn on **Developer mode**.
3. Click **Load unpacked** and select the `ext` folder (or `dist/youtube-refined`).

## Use

Pin YouTube Refined from Chrome’s Extensions menu, then click its toolbar icon
to open the settings. Turn custom grid columns on or off, and change the count
with the buttons, slider, or number field. Use the switches to filter Shorts,
Mixes, live streams, started videos, playlists, topic sections, membership sections,
and the navigation sidebar. On watch pages, you can hide comments, the sidebar,
and end-screen cards. Changes apply to open YouTube tabs right away, and all
filters are optional.
